> **For** Any person with access to the internet
> **who** Has many commitments in his life,
> **the** ICommit app **is a** Cloud based application
> **that** Makes fulfilling commitments easier.
> **Unlike** Any calendar, reminder or task management application,
> **our product** Allows people engagin to commitment to have shared understanding about the commitment, it's sub-commitments and when and how each commitment needs to be fulfilled.
