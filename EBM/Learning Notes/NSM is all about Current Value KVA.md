### An NSM is a Current Value metric by definition


A North Star Metric is _the single measure that best captures the value your product delivers to customers_. Delivered value **is** Current Value. So an NSM lands in CV essentially by construction.

You couldn't have a north star in Time to Market — T2M measures a _capability_ (how fast you can learn and ship), not value received by anyone. Same for Ability to Innovate. So the fact that everything in that file is CV isn't an omission; it's what the artifact is.

### Two artifacts, two jobs
- NSM File
	- Question
		- What's the one number that captures customer value — and how do we stop it being gamed?
	- Shape
		- Narrow and deep — one metric, its inputs, its guardrails
	- Audience
		- Product team, weekly
	- Answers
		- "What do we do next?"
- EBM scorecard
	- Question
		- Across all four dimensions (CV, UV, T2M, A2I), how healthy are we?
	- Shape
		- Broad and shallow — 1–3 measures per KVA
	- Audience
		- Leadership, quarterly
	- Answers
		- "Should we keep investing in this product?"

**The scorecard is the umbrella. The NSM is the deepest cell inside it.**
