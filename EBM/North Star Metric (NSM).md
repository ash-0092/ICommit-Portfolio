A North Star Metric is the single measure that best captures the value your product delivers to customers (Current Value -- When it's provided)
## Vision

> [A world where a promise between two people is never forgotten, and nobody has to chase.](../Product-Vision-Board-with-Checklist.pdf)

### NSM (slow, strategic, reviewed monthly or quarterly. Tells if we're winning.)

| Product | Bad NSMs                                                  | Good NSM                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ICommit | 1. Number of installs<br>2. Number of created commitments | **Promise Keepers**<br><br>**Promise Keepers** — Number of monthly active users who fulfill a shared commitment in >= 2 of the last 4 weeks<br><br>**Important note:** the `>= 2 of 4 weeks` threshold is a hypothesis — confirm natural frequency in discovery. |

### Input metrics (weekly — what we steer by — fast, weekly. Tells what to do next)

| Metric                                                          | Why                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| % of new signups who fulfill a shared commitment within 7 days  | Entry into the user habit — primary measure of the [Product Goal](../Product%20Goal.md) |
| Number of shared commitments fulfilled per active user per week | Depth of the user habit                                                                 |
| % of shared commitments fulfilled on or before due date         | Quality of the user habit                                                               |

---
### Counter-metrics (must not get worse — guardrails. Tells if we're winning dishonestly)

| Metric                                                | Floor/Ceiling                  | Guards against                 | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| % of fulfilled commitments that were two-party agreed | >= 80%                         | Drifting into a to-do list     | If majority of fulfilled commitments are **not** two-party agreed (not a shared commitment), then the differentiator diminishes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Fulfillment rate (fulfilled / created)                | >= 60%                         | Trivial-commitment inflation   | Trivial commitments get _created_ far faster than they get fulfilled (someone adds ten and finishes four). So the denominator grows faster than the numerator (fulfilled / created) and the ratio drops, even while the raw count of fulfilled commitments rises.<br>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Notification opt-out rate                             | <= 10% of monthly active users | Winning completions by nagging | Our vision says "nobody has to chase."<br><br>If the app becomes the chaser, then we haven't solved the problem -- we've relocated it from the partner to the phone, and the user experience shifts from "this helps me keep promises/fulfill commitments" to "this thing nags me".<br><br>Notifications are the _mechanism_ of nagging, so measuring how many people switch them off directly measures whether that mechanism has been overused, and **more importantly** it's early. The sequence is: annoyed → mute notifications → stop opening the app → uninstall<br><br>Opt-out is unambiguous and gives you time to react — because once users have churned, it's nearly impossible to know whether it was notifications, price, or a competitor. |

## NSM validity checklist
- [x] Single metric
- [x] Easy to understand
- [x] Customer centric
- [x] Ensures value is sustainable (Forming a habit)
- [x] Represents progress towards vision
- [x] Quantitative (Relies on numbers not opinions)
- [x] Actionable (I can take action based on how it changes)
- [x] Serves as leading indicator of the long-term business success
