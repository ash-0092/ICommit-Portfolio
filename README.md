
# Description
`Icommit` is a SaaS that helps everyone with too may commitments to manage and track their commitments easily and in a trusted way.

A user may add a commitment for himself, ask someone to ask him for a  commitment, or get assigned to a commitment by someone else from him contacts list.

Each commitment will have:
- Due date
- Signed statement of agreement between Accountable person and Requesting person
- Requesting person
- Accountable person
- Pre-commitments / Dependencies
	- Each dependency is a commitment itself
- State (Not-started / In-progress / Done)
- Remind before (how many days)

### Example of usage:
- My wife asks me visit school and provide the papers required so that our daughter gets accepted to join Year 1 stage.
- My wife adds commitment and assigns it to me.
- I review the commitment and realize that we need to prepare the papers first.
- I start add pre-commitments for preparing papers and split them between me and my wife.
- Me and my wife sign-off to each commitment so that we both know that we agree on it -- The commitment can't be marked as `in-progress` unless we both agree on it.
- The main commitment can't be marked as `in-progress` unless we both agree on it and the pre-commitments are already `Done`.
- The person assigned to commitment gets reminded by the application before each commitment according to time the creator of the commitment decides.

# Vision
Build a product that everyone who has access to smart phone or computer can use everyday to track and manage his commitments easily and never see commitments coming out of hand or be forgotten.
