# Biggest untested assumptions

Everything in the Product Vision Board and the GO Product Roadmap is currently a hypothesis. These are the assumptions that would do the most damage if they turn out to be wrong, ordered by risk.

---

## 1. The two-party sign-off is felt as *trust-building*, not as *bureaucratic friction between family members*
**← Riskiest. This is the one that kills the product if it's wrong, because the sign-off is the differentiator.**

- **Testable form:** In 10 interviews, ≥6 fathers describe a past instance where an unclear verbal agreement with a partner caused a problem, **and** ≥6 react positively when shown the sign-off flow.
- **Test:** 10 story-based interviews + a clickable Figma flow. ~2 weeks, £0.
- **If false:** the sign-off has to become implicit or lightweight rather than an explicit gate — which means rethinking the core of the product.

## 2. Fathers are the right beachhead, rather than mothers

- **Testable form:** In 10 interviews across both parents in the same households, ≥6 indicate that the father is the one who most feels the pain of untracked commitments.
- **Test:** 10 story-based interviews, ~2 weeks.
- **If false:** the target group moves to mothers, or widens to cover both parents.

## 3. Fathers experience commitment-tracking as painful enough to adopt a new app (vs. WhatsApp + memory)

- **Testable form:** In 10 interviews with fathers, ≥6 describe a specific instance in the last 30 days where a family commitment was forgotten or required chasing.
- **Test:** 10 story-based interviews, ~2 weeks.
- **If false:** find the real pain point and adapt the product goal, or accept that the problem is not severe enough to build a business on.

## 4. Someone will pay for this, and family/group packages are the primary revenue driver

- **Testable form:** In 10 interviews, ≥6 can name what they currently pay for in this category, **and** ≥3 agree to join a paid waiting list at the intended price.
- **Test:** 10 story-based interviews + a landing page with a price and a sign-up button, ~2 weeks.
- **If false:** discover what users actually value enough to pay for, or change the revenue model.

---

**Note on method:** interviews ask about *specific past behaviour*, never about hypothetical future intent ("would you use…", "would you pay…"). Stated intent is not evidence.
